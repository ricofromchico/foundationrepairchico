Chico's Foundation Repair Service

Solid Chico Foundations helps homeowners in Chico, California deal with cracked, settling, and shifting foundations. Whether the problem is a small crack or uneven floors throughout the house, call 530-456-4554 to talk about your foundation and your options.

Changes in soil moisture, drainage problems, and aging materials can all affect a foundation over time. Catching problems early usually keeps repairs simpler.

Foundation Repair Services in Chico

  • Foundation crack repair: vertical, horizontal, and stair-step cracks in foundation walls.
  • Settling and sinking foundations: stabilizing and correcting uneven settlement.
  • Pier and beam foundation repair: support, leveling, and replacement of failing components.
  • Slab foundation repair: addressing cracked, sunken, or uneven slabs.
  • Crawl space repair: support and moisture problems beneath the home.
  • Foundation leveling: correcting sloping and uneven floors.
  • Drainage and moisture control: managing water around your foundation.

Signs Your Chico Home May Need Foundation Repair

  • Cracks in walls, ceilings, brick, or the foundation itself
  • Doors and windows that stick or won't latch
  • Sloping, bouncy, or uneven floors
  • Gaps between walls and ceilings, floors, or window frames
  • Standing water or dampness in the crawl space
